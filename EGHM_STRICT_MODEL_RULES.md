# EGHM strict model rules

These rules are non-negotiable. A lower RMSE never overrides a failed physical or ecological rule.

1. **Exact daily water balance**: maximum daily closure error must be <= 1e-8 m3.
2. **lambda = 0**: no memory/relaxation/carry-over/damping surrogate may be introduced under another name.
3. **2011 is an initial/reference observation, not a cap**: no hard storage/area maximum at the 2011 state.
4. **No arbitrary spill/freeboard thresholds**: overflow or drainage thresholds require independently observed geometry; otherwise they are forbidden.
5. **There is no 2022 mapped pond-area observation in the current analysis**: 2022 is neither calibration data nor holdout data. Its meteorology may remain in the continuous forcing series, but no 2022 mapped pond area may be fitted, scored, ranked, validated against, or used for stopping/tuning.
6. **Observed mapped open-water pond-area years are fixed**: 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023. The current R1 Methods describe these observations as manually digitized water-body boundaries from 0.5-m orthorectified airborne images. Historical thesis wording that called the same series “wetland area” is provenance context, not the current observation definition.
7. **Evaluation process support is fixed to April-May**: archived image metadata place the historical orthorectified acquisitions in April or May. Until exact acquisition dates are recovered, use April 1-May 31 mean process support consistently; this support was chosen from metadata, not by minimizing RMSE.
8. **Mapped pond area, hydraulic state, and binary pool presence are distinct quantities**: (a) historical mapped open-water polygon area, (b) modeled conserved surface storage and hydraulic wetted area, and (c) seasonal visible-pool presence/disappearance are related but not interchangeable observation variables.
9. **Hydraulic zero and visible-pool absence are not synonymous**: numerical zero of conserved surface storage is a hydraulic-state diagnostic only. The present geometry does not independently constrain the water-depth/microtopography/vegetation threshold at which shallow stored water ceases to appear as a visible pool. No arbitrary threshold may be fitted merely to reproduce desired dry timing.
10. **Seasonal surface-pool drying is an external hydroperiod diagnostic**: field and remote-sensing evidence may test whether recurrent spring exposure/drying and subsequent rewetting are plausible. Monthly NDWI does not establish an exact annual dry-day target, and dry-day count is not a calibration gate.
11. **No future information**: predictors must be causal/antecedent only. No centered rolling windows, negative shifts, future leakage, or use of later-year observations as predictors.
12. **No explicit time trend**: year/date may index observations but may not be a fitted predictor of mapped pond area.
13. **Process accounting**: every new hydrologic loss/gain must enter the conserved water balance as an explicit flux and be recorded in diagnostics.
14. **New-process identifiability**: if a stage is proposed to test a new process, the accepted candidate must use that process materially. A coefficient driven effectively to zero is evidence to remove or redesign the process, not a reason to preserve an arbitrary tiny nonzero value.
15. **Short-term hydrologic term**: while the mapped pond-area observation operator explicitly contains a short-term hydrologic term, its accepted coefficient must be >0. If later removed by model redesign, removal must be explicit.
16. **No grid-edge acceptance**: a calibrated search parameter at the minimum or maximum tested guard value is not accepted as final. Expand/refine the guard range and rerun. Internal OAT support endpoints are sensitivity bookkeeping and are not themselves calibration guard bounds.
17. **Accuracy gate**: full-six-observation mapped pond-area nRMSE must be <=2.0% after all physical/ecological gates pass. Leave-one-year-out and nested-CV scores are optional diagnostics only and are not acceptance or ranking gates.
18. **Ecological trend guard**: the ecological state must have |corr(state, year)| <0.99 over the six observed years; otherwise it is treated as an implicit time-trend surrogate.
19. **The six observed years form one calibration/evaluation set**: do not repeatedly redefine the data set by hiding one year and then reject a physically acceptable model because its fitted coefficients change. Such leave-one-year-out instability may be reported as a small-sample diagnostic, not as a model-validity gate.
20. **Literature-supported structure, site-calibrated parameters**: distinguish a process supported by literature from a parameter actually measured at Seoyeongari. Do not label calibrated values as measured.
21. **Report order** after substantive mapped pond-area runs: full-six RMSE and nRMSE first, then water-balance/area/precipitation closure, physical/ecological gates, year-wise predictions, parameter interpretation, sensitivity results, and optional CV diagnostics only if useful. Hydroperiod diagnostics are reported separately under their own observation semantics.
22. **No spatial double counting**: every square metre of the modeled hydrologic domain must belong to exactly one precipitation/ET footprint on each day. Overlapping upland + wetland/open-water forcing domains are forbidden.
23. **Area partition closure**: the daily non-overlapping area components must sum to the fixed modeled domain within 1e-8 m2.
24. **Precipitation partition closure**: the sum of rainfall volumes assigned to the daily area components must equal precipitation depth times the modeled domain within 1e-8 m3.
25. **Geometry bookkeeping is not calibration**: the 2011 reference pond footprint, external contributing area and potential wetland footprint must be reconciled explicitly. Small vector/raster area discrepancies are reported, not tuned away to improve fit.
26. **No arbitrary within-day loss priority**: surface evaporation, surface drainage and subsurface leakage acting during the same daily time step must be evaluated from a common pre-loss surface state. If their combined potential loss exceeds available storage, scale concurrent losses proportionally.
27. **Nested model-selection validation is not mandatory** under the six-observation analysis. Historical Stage39-48 nested results remain archived but do not determine acceptance of the current model.
28. **Fixed-candidate LOOCV is diagnostic only**: refitting Kc/Kh after omitting one of six observations may be inspected for sensitivity but must not determine the selected physical/ecological structure.
29. **Ecological coefficient plausibility outranks tiny numerical gains**: coefficients and process time scales must be interpretable and defensible. A near-zero fitted rate introduced only to satisfy a nonzero gate must be removed or reformulated rather than presented as a meaningful ecological process.
30. **Sensitivity analysis tests robustness, not desired ranking**: use ecologically/hydrologically defensible perturbation ranges and the same stated rules across competing model structures. Report whether the Integrated model remains superior or whether rankings overlap; do not choose ranges solely to force a preferred winner.
31. **Calibration-dimensionality warning**: six mapped pond-area target years cannot independently identify every hydrologic and ecological parameter. Prefer literature/field constraints where available, and describe remaining fitted parameters explicitly as calibrated rather than measured.
32. **Observation provenance conflicts must be resolved in favor of the current analysis definition, with history retained**: the undergraduate thesis described the historical polygon series more broadly as wetland-area change during terrestrialization, whereas the current R1 Methods explicitly describe manual digitization of water-body boundaries to quantify open-water pond area. The latter is the active EGHM target definition; the former must not be silently substituted into model validation.
